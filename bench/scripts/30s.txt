Welcome to the Northwind Dealer Portal. To accept your invitation, click "Accept invitation" on the email from Northwind Motors. On the setup page, enter your full name, then choose "Pacific Northwest" from the Dealer region list. Your password needs at least 12 characters. If the two passwords don't match, an error appears under the form, so check both fields and try again. Click "Create account", and you'll see the message: your account is ready.
