In this walkthrough, you'll set up single sign-on for your dealership in the Northwind Admin Console. It takes about five minutes, and you'll need an administrator account.
Start on the Admin Console home page. In the left navigation, open "Security", then select "Single sign-on". If you don't see the Security section, ask your account owner to grant you the Administrator role.
Northwind supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. For this example, we'll use SAML, which works with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, and Google Workspace.
Click "Add identity provider". In the "Display name" field, type a name your team will recognize, such as "Dealer SSO". Then copy the Entity ID and the Assertion Consumer Service URL. You'll paste both values into your identity provider in a moment.
Switch to your identity provider and create a new SAML application. Paste the Entity ID into the "Audience URI" field, and the ACS URL into the "Single sign-on URL" field. Set the Name ID format to email address. When you're done, download the metadata XML file.
Back in Northwind, click "Upload metadata" and choose the XML file you just downloaded. The console reads the certificate and shows its expiry date. In our example, the certificate expires on March 14, 2027, at 11:59 PM UTC.
Next, map your attributes. Northwind needs three values: email, first name and last name. If your provider sends a department attribute, you can map it as well, which lets you route orders to the right team automatically.
Now choose who signs in with SSO. Select "Require SSO for all users in this domain", then enter your domain, for example northwind-dealers dot com. Users with personal email addresses will keep using their password and two-factor authentication.
Before you turn it on, click "Test connection". A new window opens and asks you to sign in through your identity provider. If the test succeeds, you'll see a green check mark and the message "Connection verified". If it fails, the console shows the error code, such as SAML-401, along with a link to the troubleshooting guide.
Finally, click "Enable SSO". The change applies within about 60 seconds. Anyone who is already signed in stays signed in until their session ends, which is 8 hours by default.
To review sign-in activity later, open "Security", then "Audit log". You can filter by date, user, or event type, and export the results as a CSV file with up to 10,000 rows.
That's it. Your dealership now signs in with single sign-on, and new team members get access as soon as your identity provider assigns them to the Northwind application.
